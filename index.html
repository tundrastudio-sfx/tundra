04 / About the studio

A sound studio for advertising and film.

Tundra is a sound studio founded by Jerónimo Sibuet in Buenos Aires. We work alongside production companies and agencies from picture lock to final delivery: sound design, foley, original music, dialogue edit and mix.

Our sound has run on campaigns for Adidas, EA Sports FC and Kinderland, on Bizarrap's Bizapop, and on teasers and live show intros for the region's biggest artists.
